Approving lets the app act on your workspace. It can:

  • Upload files and create share links, list your artifacts, and revoke them
  • See your plan, usage, and account email address

It cannot create, list, or revoke API keys, disconnect your other apps, or touch billing or your account settings.

  • Access lasts until you disconnect it: the app renews its access while in use, and it lapses after about two weeks unused
  • Disconnect it any time under Connected apps on your dashboard
