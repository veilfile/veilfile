# Report abuse

If you believe a file hosted on Veilfile violates our terms, tell us. Include the link to the file and a short description of what you found. You can report anonymously, but leaving contact information helps us follow up.

Reports of child sexual abuse material are prioritized above everything else. We remove confirmed abusive content and report apparent child sexual exploitation to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children, as federal law requires.

Email [abuse@veilfile.com](mailto:abuse@veilfile.com). We acknowledge reports within one business day.
